# Figure 1. SpatialESS preserves paired numerical outputs and extends analysis to larger spatial inputs.

**(A)** Author-provided SpatialESS workflow. The original vector PDF is placed at full figure width with its aspect ratio and internal content unchanged.

**(B)** Probability agreement for the largest completed paired CosMx benchmark (50,000 cells). Axes show log10-transformed positive probabilities; hexagon color and marginal bars indicate record counts. Pearson correlation is calculated on the original probability scale; maximum absolute error is reported in probability units.

**(C)** Numerical agreement across six completed paired CosMx subsets. Rows show probability correlation, maximum absolute probability difference, exact permutation-p-value fraction, active-record Jaccard and significance-call disagreements. The additional Atera validation comprised 10 paired breast and cervical fixtures from 1,000 to 50,000 cells using 18,028 measured genes, 3,081 fixed ligand–receptor records and 100 permutations. All 10 met the prespecified strict criterion: active-record Jaccard = 1, permutation p values identical, no significance-call disagreements and maximum absolute probability error 2.98 × 10−16. Displayed CosMx correlations rounded to 1 do not imply exact probability equality.

**(D)** Whole-process runtime across CosMx CancerousLiver and Atera breast and cervical inputs. Blue circles identify SpatialESS and orange squares identify SpatialCellChat. Solid, dashed and dotted lines distinguish CosMx, Atera breast and Atera cervical, respectively. Pale grey shading marks input sizes with completed measurements from both methods. Lines connect measured successful runs only. SpatialCellChat completed paired Atera comparisons through 50,000 cells; its 100,000-cell attempts failed during allocation under the configured memory budget. A reference attempt on the full 715,512-cell cervical input failed while allocating a 96.1 GiB dense intermediate under the 96 GiB virtual-memory limit (31.89 s; peak RSS 12.96 GB), so no full-scale reference result was generated. The full-breast reference was not attempted. SpatialESS completed the full 169,940-cell breast and 715,512-cell cervical inputs. CosMx SpatialESS values at 50,000 and 443,515 cells are medians of three repetitions; other displayed values are single measurements or saved reference measurements.

**(E)** Peak resident memory for the same runs, with method colors and cohort line styles as in D. CosMx measurements recorded in GiB are converted to decimal GB; Atera memory is reported in decimal GB. Failed attempts are not plotted as successful measurements.

**(F)** Potential ordered cell pairs (N²) and observed directed non-self radius-graph edges (E) across 14 CosMx, Xenium and Visium HD inputs. The shared F–H legend identifies platform and sampling design; the dashed grey curve denotes N², and only the nested CosMx edge series is connected.

**(G)** Mean outgoing spatial degree (E/N) across the same 14 inputs. The shared F–H legend identifies the platform and sampling design; point labels give fixture and scale. These are individual fixtures, not replicate uncertainty estimates.

**(H)** Radius-graph density (E/N²) for the same inputs and shared F–H legend. The effective radius was 35 μm. Cluster-stratified Xenium subsets show the results for spatially dispersed sampling.

**(I)** Completion status across CosMx and Atera scales. Tick labels show 1k, 5k, 10k, 25k, 50k, 100k, 200k and 700k, with 200k followed directly by 700k. The 400k cervical and 443,515-cell (nominal 450k) CosMx categories are omitted from this panel; their run records remain in the source data, and the 443,515-cell CosMx measurements remain in D/E. Exact cell counts and status provenance are provided in the source data. At 200k, cervical SpatialESS was completed; SpatialCellChat had allocation failure under the configured memory budget. The 169,940-cell Atera breast category and two 250-cell CosMx points are also omitted from I, with run records retained. Filled circles indicate paired completion, open circles indicate SpatialESS-only completion, crosses indicate failed SpatialCellChat attempts. The cross at 700k uses the same reference-failed symbol as the earlier failed attempts. The run failed requesting a 96.1 GiB dense intermediate; the source data retain its status and resource measurements. At 100,000 cells, both Atera reference attempts failed allocation under the configured memory budget; the full-breast reference was not attempted. These budget-specific outcomes do not establish a universal limit for SpatialCellChat.
