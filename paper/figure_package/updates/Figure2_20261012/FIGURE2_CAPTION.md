Figure 2. Patient-aware analysis of communication scores in pulmonary fibrosis. GSE250346 contains 45 slices from 35 individuals: 10 control slices from 9 individuals and 35 pulmonary-fibrosis slices from 26 individuals.

(A) Mixed-effects model associations, plotted as condition effects against -log10(Benjamini-Hochberg q). Eight labelled features retain their stable identifiers and full molecular and sender-to-receiver identities. Effects represent pulmonary fibrosis relative to control on the log1p-score scale.

(B) Twelve selected condition effects with 95% Wald confidence intervals. Open symbols indicate valid singular fits; the reference line marks zero effect.

(C) Twenty-eight selected features compared between the patient mixed-effects model and slice-level ordinary least squares. Colour indicates standardized effect, defined as the condition coefficient divided by the across-slice standard deviation of the zero-inclusive log1p score; dots indicate q < 0.05. Nonzero-slice fraction is the number of slices with a positive score divided by 45. Patient ICC describes the fraction of total fitted variance attributed to the patient random intercept. Both annotation bars range from 0 to 1. Grey squares in the Singular column identify valid singular boundary fits.

(D) Zero-inclusive slice scores and patient means for I15, FN1-(ITGAV+ITGB1), Subpleural FBs to Subpleural FBs; I06, COL1A1-(ITGA3+ITGB1), Myofibroblasts to Myofibroblasts; and I19, HLA-DRA-CD4, pDCs to pDCs. Small points indicate slices; outlined points indicate patient means of log1p(slice score). Labels below Control and PF report nonzero slices divided by all slices in that condition: 2/10 and 30/35 for I15, 5/10 and 34/35 for I06, and 1/10 and 34/35 for I19. Tick values are displayed in units of 0.001. Jitter affects horizontal position only.

(E) Selected pathway and cell-pair representatives. Each bubble represents one ligand-receptor feature; colour indicates standardized effect and area indicates capped -log10(q). These feature-level associations do not constitute a pathway-enrichment test. CSPG4 is displayed as CSPG4-(ITGA3+ITGB1), with its original feature key and statistics retained.

Valid singular fits are included in the primary Benjamini-Hochberg correction; convergence-warning and failed fits receive no primary q values. Complete feature identities, values and support counts are supplied in Figure2_Source_Data.xlsx.
