# TSTA saved feasibility evidence

All 1,258 sample summaries/status/input manifests, metadata, source, scripts,
available logs, and two representative folders are retained. Raw matrices are
from https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17664023 and are not copied into this package.
No paired SpatialCellChat validation was performed. Samples are not patients.

Mount the companion with `tools/reproduction.py`. The copied TSTA_0117 records
stream is truncated and unusable; it is preserved as received. TSTA_0001 records
are usable. Aggregate completion reports describe the historical run, not a
new validation of every copied output. See the [scope and limitations](../../docs/REPRODUCE_20261023.md).
