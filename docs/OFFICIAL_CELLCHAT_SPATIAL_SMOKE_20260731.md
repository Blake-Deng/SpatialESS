# Official CellChat spatial comparator smoke test

Date: 2026-07-31

This controlled test verifies that both complete spatial workflows execute on
the same input before a public biological dataset is used. It is an
integration and formula test, not biological evidence.

## Fixed input

- 600 cells on a regular 5-micrometer grid;
- three adjacent spatial groups;
- 12 simple interactions selected from `CellChatDB.human`;
- identical normalized expression, group labels and coordinates;
- CellChat type-7 triMean and 20 bootstraps;
- 20-micrometer interaction length;
- official CellChat 1.6.1 spatial workflow;
- SpatialESS binary CSR radius graph.

## Results

| Metric | Official CellChat spatial | SpatialESS |
|---|---:|---:|
| Supported group pairs | 7 | 7 |
| Active LR-group records | 84 | 84 |
| Strict-tail p <= 0.05 | 36 | 36 |
| Process wall time | 4.73 s | 4.05 s |
| Peak RSS | 567,768 kB | 458,216 kB |

The supported-pair Jaccard index was 1.0. After dividing the official CellChat
probability by its documented `P.spatial` factor, all 84 shared molecular
probabilities agreed with a maximum absolute difference of
`1.66533453693773e-16` and Pearson correlation 1.0. Weighted spatial score
rank correlation was 1.0 in this controlled geometry.

The two independent global strict-tail permutation implementations had p-value
Spearman correlation 0.986 and no disagreements at the 0.05 threshold. Their
raw p-values need not be identical because their random permutation streams
are generated by different algorithms. The new spatial-block calibrated mode
identified 33 records at p <= 0.05 and is intentionally reported separately.
